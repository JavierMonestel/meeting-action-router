How it works

  1. Parse speakers, timestamps and who is external (role + organization).
  2. Extract commitments, decisions and open questions: Claude with a typed schema, or the rules engine as fallback.
  3. Ground every item in a verbatim quote and timestamp.
  4. Route to Linear, Attio, Notion or Calendar, and show the exact API request.
  5. Review & send: a human approves, then items are created (or dry-run).
