Reading these numbers

The rules engine is close to perfect on the meetings it was built against and drops to about half on new phrasing ("Action item for me: …", "Let me log …", third-person commitments). That gap is exactly why the production path is Claude with a typed schema, with the rules engine kept as a zero-cost fallback. To score Claude on the same sets:

ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=... python -m evals.run --engine claude
